Our Responsibility

Environmental Statement

Our Commitment

Judith Ethnic Food Store Ltd recognises the importance of reducing environmental impact. We are committed to operating our business in a way that is mindful of the environment and the communities we serve.

Responsible Practices

Where reasonably possible, we aim to:

  • reduce unnecessary waste;
  • encourage responsible packaging practices;
  • improve operational efficiency;
  • comply with environmental requirements.

Future Improvements

We will continue to explore ways to improve our environmental responsibility while providing quality products and services to our customers. As our business grows, we remain committed to reviewing and improving our environmental practices.
