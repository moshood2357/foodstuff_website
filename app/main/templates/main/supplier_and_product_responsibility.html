Supplier & Product Responsibility Statement

Our Commitment

Judith Ethnic Food Store Ltd aims to provide customers with quality products sourced from trusted suppliers. We are committed to responsible product selection and customer satisfaction.

Product Selection

When selecting products, we consider:

  • product quality;
  • customer demand;
  • supplier reliability;
  • food safety requirements.

Food Safety

We expect suppliers to provide products that meet applicable food safety standards and provide accurate product information. Food safety is a priority in our product selection and supplier relationships.

Continuous Improvement

We regularly review our products and services to improve customer satisfaction. We welcome feedback from customers about product quality so we can continue to raise our standards.
