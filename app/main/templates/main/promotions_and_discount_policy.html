Promotions & Discount Policy

Effective Date: 1 August 2026

1 Introduction

At Judith Ethnic Food Store Ltd, we regularly offer promotions, discounts, special offers, and customer rewards to provide value to our customers. This policy explains the terms that apply to promotional offers available through our website, physical store, email marketing, social media channels, and other approved promotional platforms.

2 Types of Promotions

Promotional offers may include discount codes, percentage discounts, fixed-value discounts, seasonal offers, multi-buy offers, clearance offers, loyalty rewards, special customer promotions, and free delivery offers. Each promotion may have its own specific terms.

3 Discount Codes

Where discount codes are offered, customers must enter the correct code at checkout, use the code before its expiry date, and meet any stated conditions. Discount codes may apply only to selected products, require a minimum order value, be limited to one use per customer, or exclude certain products.

4 Promotional Periods

All promotions are available only during the stated promotional period. We reserve the right to start or end promotions at any time, extend promotional periods, or withdraw offers where necessary.

5 Product Exclusions

Some promotions may exclude already discounted products, limited-stock items, specific brands, selected categories, or delivery charges. Any exclusions will be clearly communicated.

6 Multi-Buy Offers

For offers such as Buy One Get One Free, Buy Two for £X, or Buy More Save More, customers must purchase the required quantity to receive the offer. If products are returned or refunded, the discount may be adjusted accordingly.

7 Loyalty Rewards

If we operate a loyalty scheme, rewards may have expiry dates, points or benefits may not be exchanged for cash, rewards may be subject to minimum spending requirements, and accounts may be cancelled where misuse is identified. Further loyalty scheme terms may apply.

8 Gift Cards and Vouchers

Where gift cards or vouchers are available, they must be used before expiry, cannot normally be exchanged for cash, may only be used for eligible purchases, and lost vouchers may not be replaced unless proof of purchase is provided. Additional gift card terms may apply.

9 Pricing Errors

We aim to ensure all promotional prices are accurate. If a pricing error occurs, we reserve the right to correct the price, contact customers before completing the order, or cancel the order and provide a refund if necessary.

10 Availability

Promotional products are subject to availability. We may limit quantities available, purchases per customer, and promotional access.

11 Combining Offers

Unless stated otherwise, promotional codes cannot be combined, only one discount may apply per transaction, and promotions cannot be exchanged for cash.

12 Abuse of Promotions

We reserve the right to refuse promotional benefits where we believe there is fraudulent activity, misuse of discount codes, creation of multiple accounts to abuse offers, or resale or commercial misuse of promotions.

13 Refunds and Promotions

Where an order containing promotional discounts is refunded, the refund amount may reflect the promotional discount applied, free gifts may need to be returned where applicable, and loyalty points may be adjusted.

14 Marketing Consent

Customers who receive promotional communications by email or other channels can unsubscribe at any time. Stopping marketing communications will not affect essential order messages.
