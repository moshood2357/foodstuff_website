Legal & Policies

Social Media Policy

1 Introduction

Judith Ethnic Food Store Ltd uses social media channels to communicate with customers, share updates, and promote products.

2 Customer Interaction

We welcome questions, feedback, product suggestions, and comments. We aim to respond professionally and respectfully to all customer interactions on our social media channels.

3 Acceptable Behaviour

Customers should not post:

  • abusive comments;
  • threats;
  • discriminatory content;
  • spam;
  • misleading information.

4 Moderation

We reserve the right to remove content that violates our community standards and to restrict access to users who repeatedly breach this policy.

5 Privacy

Customers should avoid sharing personal information publicly through social media. Private matters — such as order queries, complaints, or personal details — should be discussed through our appropriate contact channels to protect your privacy.
