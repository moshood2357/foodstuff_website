Legal & Policies

Cookie Policy

Effective Date: 1 August 2026

1 Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Judith Ethnic Food Store Ltd ("we", "our", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit judithethnicstore.co.uk. This policy explains what cookies are, why we use them, what types of cookies we may use, and how you can manage your cookie preferences. This Cookie Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

2 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They allow websites to remember your actions and preferences, provide essential functionality, improve your browsing experience, and understand how visitors use the website. Cookies do not normally contain information that directly identifies you.

3 How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to help us operate our website, remember your shopping basket, process online orders, improve website performance, understand customer behaviour, provide a better shopping experience, and support security and fraud prevention.

4 Types of Cookies We Use

4.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for our website to function. They may support shopping basket functionality, account login, checkout processes, website security, and payment processing. These cookies cannot normally be disabled because the website may not function correctly without them.

4.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make, such as preferred language, delivery preferences, saved settings, and recently viewed products. They help provide a more personalised experience.

4.3 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website. They may collect information such as number of visitors, pages viewed, time spent on pages, and navigation patterns. We use this information to improve website design, product presentation, and customer experience. Analytics information is generally collected in an aggregated form.

4.4 Marketing Cookies

Where you give consent, marketing cookies may be used to understand your interests, measure advertising performance, and show relevant promotions. These cookies may be provided by trusted third-party services. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

5 Third-Party Cookies

Some services used on our website may place cookies on your device — including payment providers, website analytics services, social media platforms, and advertising services. These providers have their own privacy and cookie policies.

6 Managing Cookie Preferences

When you first visit our website, you may be asked to choose your cookie preferences. You can accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or manage your preferences. You can also control cookies through your internet browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, delete, or block cookies and receive warnings before cookies are stored. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

7 Cookies and Online Shopping

Cookies help provide essential online shopping functions, including keeping products in your basket, remembering customer login sessions, completing checkout, and maintaining secure transactions. Without certain cookies, online ordering may not work correctly.

8 Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on judithethnicstore.co.uk.
