Legal & Policies

Modern Slavery Statement

Introduction

Judith Ethnic Food Store Ltd is committed to operating responsibly and ethically. We do not tolerate slavery, forced labour, human trafficking, or exploitation of any kind.

Our Approach

We aim to work with suppliers and partners who share our commitment to ethical business practices. We expect suppliers to comply with applicable laws and maintain responsible working practices.

Supplier Responsibility

Where appropriate, we consider:

  • supplier reputation;
  • ethical sourcing;
  • compliance with legal requirements.

Our Commitment

We continue to review our practices and improve our approach to responsible business operations. We take our obligations seriously and will act on any concerns raised relating to modern slavery within our supply chain or operations.
