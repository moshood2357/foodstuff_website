Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

Who is Judith Ethnic Food Store Ltd?

Judith Ethnic Food Store Ltd is an African and international grocery retailer based in the United Kingdom, providing customers with quality food products, traditional ingredients, and everyday grocery essentials.

What products do you sell?

We sell a wide range of products, including:

  • African food products
  • International groceries
  • Spices and seasonings
  • Traditional ingredients
  • Packaged foods
  • Household essentials
  • Other everyday grocery items

Our product range changes regularly based on customer demand and availability.

Do you have a physical store?

Yes. Customers can visit our physical store to browse and purchase products in person.

Address: 311 High Street, Cheltenham, England, GL50 3HW
Opening Hours: Monday – Saturday: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM  |  Sunday: Closed

Online Shopping

Can I order products online?

Yes. You can browse products, place orders, and select available delivery or collection options through our website: judithethnicstore.co.uk.

How do I place an order?
  1. Select your products and add them to your basket.
  2. Review your order.
  3. Enter your delivery or collection details.
  4. Complete payment.
  5. Receive your order confirmation.
Can I change or cancel my order?

Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel an order. Once an order has been prepared, changes or cancellations may not always be possible — particularly for fresh, chilled, or frozen products.

Delivery

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. Delivery options may be available depending on your location and the products ordered. Available delivery options and any charges will be shown during checkout.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times vary depending on your location, delivery method, order volume, and product availability. Estimated delivery information will be provided during checkout.

What happens if my delivery arrives damaged?

Please contact us as soon as possible and provide:

  • Your order number
  • Details of the problem
  • Photographs where possible

We will investigate and provide an appropriate solution.

Click & Collect

Do you offer Click & Collect?

Yes. Where available, you can order online and collect from our store. You will receive collection instructions after your order has been confirmed.

What do I need when collecting my order?

When collecting, you may need:

  • Your order confirmation
  • Your order number
  • The name used for the order
  • Identification where required
Can someone else collect my order?

Yes. Another person may collect your order if they have your permission and the required order details. Additional verification may be required for security purposes.

Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

Available payment methods may include:

  • Debit cards
  • Credit cards
  • Contactless payments
  • Online payment services
  • Cash payments in-store

Payment options may vary.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. Payments are processed through secure payment systems. We do not store complete payment card details.

Products

Do you provide allergy information?

Yes. We provide allergen information where available. Customers with allergies should always check product packaging and contact us before purchasing if they need further information.

Do you sell imported African products?

Yes. We stock African and international products sourced from trusted suppliers. Product availability may vary due to supplier availability and seasonal demand.

Can I request a product?

We welcome customer suggestions. If there is a product you would like us to stock, please contact us and we will consider your request.

Returns & Refunds

Can I return food products?

Due to food safety requirements, we cannot normally accept returns of:

  • Fresh food
  • Chilled goods
  • Frozen goods
  • Opened food products

Please contact us if there is a problem with your order and we will do our best to help.

What if I receive the wrong item?

Please contact us promptly with your order details, the product information, and details of the issue. We will investigate and provide a suitable solution.

How long do refunds take?

Approved refunds are normally processed using the original payment method. The time taken for funds to appear depends on your bank or payment provider.

Your Account

Do I need an account to shop online?

Not necessarily. Where guest checkout is available, you can place orders without creating an account.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Use the password reset option on the website, or contact us for assistance.
