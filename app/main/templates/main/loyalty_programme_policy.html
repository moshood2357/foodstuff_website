Loyalty Programme Policy

1 Introduction

Judith Ethnic Food Store Ltd may introduce customer loyalty rewards, points, or membership benefits from time to time. This policy explains the general terms that apply to any loyalty programme operated by us.

2 Joining the Loyalty Programme

Where available, customers may join by creating an account, providing required customer information, and accepting the loyalty programme terms. Membership is subject to accurate customer information being provided.

3 Loyalty Rewards

Rewards may include:

  • points earned on purchases;
  • member-only discounts;
  • promotional benefits;
  • special offers.

The details of rewards may change from time to time.

4 Use of Rewards

Rewards:

  • cannot normally be exchanged for cash;
  • may have expiry dates;
  • may have minimum purchase requirements;
  • may not be transferred unless stated otherwise.

5 Misuse of Loyalty Benefits

We reserve the right to suspend or cancel accounts where we identify fraudulent activity, abuse of rewards, or creation of multiple accounts to gain unfair benefits.

6 Changes to the Loyalty Programme

We may change or end the loyalty programme by providing reasonable notice where appropriate.
